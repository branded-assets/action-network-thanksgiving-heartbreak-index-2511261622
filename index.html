Methodology

The Action Network analyzed 780 FBS-versus-FBS college football games played from Thanksgiving Thursday through Saturday between 2013 and 2025. The sample covered 137 programs, of which 115 played at least 10 eligible games and qualified for the national rankings.

Two separate indices measure qualifying losses (heartbreak) and wins (triumph). Each game receives points for its highest-priority applicable category:

5Pregame upset
4Third-quarter reversal
3Overtime result
2Close result

A pregame upset requires both the selected historical spread and multi-provider median to identify the eventual loser as a favorite of at least three points. A third-quarter reversal is a loss after leading, or a win after trailing, at the end of the third quarter. Close results are decided by three points or fewer.

Index score = 100 × weighted points ÷ (5 × eligible games). Rankings use unrounded scores; tied scores share a rank.

The indices measure historical game outcomes, not fan sentiment or the probability of a 2026 result.

Source: CollegeFootballData.com (game results, quarter scores and historical pregame point spreads). Analysis: The Action Network.
